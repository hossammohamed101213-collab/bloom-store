# Beauty collection artwork

Tool: built-in image_gen. Assets are for the current demo project, with source files preserved. Optimized files: beauty-business.webp (makeup), beauty-affiliate.webp (haircare), beauty-store.webp (retained skincare art from the accepted previous version). The three category illustrations are intentionally shared by nine demo product listings. Images do not establish real ingredients, brands or product availability.

## Makeup prompt
Premium cosmetics product artwork for a black and silver ecommerce card. Photoreal studio still life of unbranded luxury makeup: a satin black lipstick open showing muted rose pigment, brushed silver compact with nude pressed powder, and a black mascara tube with brush. Elegant reflective black glass platform, deep black background, subtle violet edge light, refined silver reflections. Wide 3:2 composition, products center-right, clean negative space left. Crisp tactile materials, premium editorial cosmetics photography. No text, labels, logos, people, UI or card borders.

## Haircare prompt
Premium haircare product artwork for a black and silver ecommerce card. Photoreal studio still life of unbranded black glass shampoo bottle with silver pump, black hair mask jar with silver lid, and small clear hair oil bottle containing pale gold oil. Elegant reflective black glass platform, deep black background, subtle mint edge light, refined silver reflections. Wide 3:2 composition, products center-right, clean negative space left. Crisp tactile realistic materials, luxury editorial beauty photography. No text, labels, logos, people, UI or card borders.

The retained skincare image prompt is documented in generation-prompts.md in this directory.
